# Start here

This guide is yours: how to make a report design and a custom cover that look right and pass every check. It ships with the Report Designer and is attached to every run. Never copy its pages into a design or a cover [src: web:https://docs.secureagentry.net/runner/].

Read it at these steps.

**A Design run:**

1. [The design document](../design/the-document.md): every field, what it does on the page, and the rules it is held to.
2. [The closed lists](../design/the-lists.md): the faces, styles and tokens a field may take. A name not on a list is refused.
3. [Choosing a look](../design/choosing-a-look.md): from a brief, a logo, references and pages to colours, faces and a cover style.
4. [Designing from a document](../design/from-a-document.md), when the person gave a document: what to look at, and how its colours, faces, running text, title page, brand words and logo become the design.
5. [Before you finish](../review/before-you-finish.md).

**A Cover run:**

1. [A custom cover's conventions](../cover/conventions.md): the function the template calls, the facts it gives you, the files you may read, and what makes an export pass a cover over. Read it in full before you write a line.
2. [A short course in cover design](../cover/a-short-course.md): composition, hierarchy, type and colour on one page.
3. [A worked example](../cover/worked-example.md): a whole cover that compiles, to start from.
4. [Before you finish](../review/before-you-finish.md).

Every render is one of twelve. Plan before the first, look hard at each, and change one thing at a time when you are close.
