# Start here

This guide is yours: how to write a good Writing Voice. It ships with the Voice Writer and is attached to every run. Never quote it in a voice and never copy its pages into one: a voice is made from the person's samples alone [src: web:https://docs.secureagentry.net/agentry/voice/].

Read it at these steps:

1. **Before the first sample**, [The dimensions of a voice](../voice/dimensions.md): what to notice as you read, and how to say it as an instruction.
2. **When a sample is a web page**, [Reading a web page](../voice/web-pages.md): the person's own writing on it, not the site around it.
3. **Before you write VOICE.md**, [A worked example](../voice/worked-example.md): a few sentences of a sample and the lines of a voice they support.
4. **Before you write VOICE.md, every time**, [What a voice never says](../review/what-a-voice-never-says.md): the questions AI Review asks of a voice before a person's account uses it. A voice that answers yes to any is refused there.

A voice is read by another model before it writes for this person. Every rule here serves that model: instructions it can follow, specific enough to change what it writes, and about style alone [src: web:https://docs.secureagentry.net/agentry/voice/].
