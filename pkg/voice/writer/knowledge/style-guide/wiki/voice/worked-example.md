# A worked example

A sample, a few sentences of a post:

> We shipped it. Late, and smaller than planned. But it works, and people use it. I'd rather that than a perfect thing nobody has seen.

What it shows, and the instructions it supports [src: web:https://docs.secureagentry.net/agentry/voice/]:

- Short sentences and fragments ("Late, and smaller than planned."): *Write short sentences. A fragment is fine for emphasis.*
- A conjunction opening a sentence ("But it works"): *You may open a sentence with "But" or "And".*
- A contraction ("I'd"): *Use contractions.*
- A plain admission before the point: *Say the bad news plainly, then what matters about it.*
- A closing preference, first person: *End on what you would choose, in the first person.*

What it does not show: paragraph length, headings, lists, how the writer disagrees, punctuation beyond commas and full stops. Leave those out until a sample shows them. One sample is a hint; a habit is what several samples share.
