# Hamburg depot

The Hamburg depot opens at 05:30, the earliest opening time in the network, and its cold room holds 25 pallets [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

## Facts

- Opens at 05:30 [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
- Cold room: 25 pallets, smaller than the 40 pallets at [Rotterdam depot](rotterdam-depot.md) [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
- No stated cold-room temperature range; the 2–8 °C figure is given only for Rotterdam *(unverified)* [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
