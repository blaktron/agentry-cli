# Gdansk depot

The Gdansk depot opened in 2025 and handles dry goods only, so it has no chilled operation of its own [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

## Facts

- Opened in 2025, the newest depot in the network [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
- Dry goods only — no cold storage role, unlike [Rotterdam depot](rotterdam-depot.md) and [Hamburg depot](hamburg-depot.md) [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
- Opening hours are not stated in the source documents *(unverified)* [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
