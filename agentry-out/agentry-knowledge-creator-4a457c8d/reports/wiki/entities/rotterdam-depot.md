# Rotterdam depot

The Rotterdam depot is a cold-chain site: it opens at 06:00 and closes at 22:00, and its cold room holds 40 pallets kept between 2 and 8 °C [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

## Facts

- Opening hours: 06:00–22:00 [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
- Cold room: 40 pallets at 2–8 °C [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
- It is one of two depots in the network with a cold room, alongside [Hamburg depot](hamburg-depot.md) [src: doc:docs/depots.md]
