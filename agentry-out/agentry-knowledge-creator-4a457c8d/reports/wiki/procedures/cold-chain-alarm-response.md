# Cold-chain alarm response

A cold-chain alarm is raised by a sustained temperature breach, and the response is a pallet move plus a call to facilities, with every alarm written up in the incident book [src: doc:docs/alarms.md].

## Trigger

At the [Rotterdam depot](../entities/rotterdam-depot.md), the alarm fires when the cold room passes 8 °C and stays there for ten minutes — beyond the 2–8 °C range that room is built to hold [src: doc:docs/alarms.md; doc:docs/depots.md].

## Steps at Rotterdam

1. The shift lead moves the pallets to the backup room [src: doc:docs/alarms.md].
2. The shift lead calls facilities on extension 4410 [src: doc:docs/alarms.md].

## Who responds

Responsibility differs by site: at Rotterdam it is the shift lead on duty, while at the [Hamburg depot](../entities/hamburg-depot.md) the alarm pages the night manager [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]. Those roles exist because of how the sites are staffed, described in [staffing and shifts](staffing-and-shifts.md) [src: doc:docs/alarms.md; doc:docs/staff.md].

## Logging

Every alarm is logged in the incident book within the hour, at any depot [src: doc:docs/alarms.md].
