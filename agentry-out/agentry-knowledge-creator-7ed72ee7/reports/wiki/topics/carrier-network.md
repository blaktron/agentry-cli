# Carrier network

Three carriers move goods between the depots, two of them chilled: Nordfreight, Van Dijk and Baltic Reefer [src: docs/carriers.md]. Every carrier must present a temperature log at hand-over, including the dry-goods operator [src: docs/carriers.md].

## Lanes and schedules

- **Nordfreight** — chilled goods between [Rotterdam](../entities/rotterdam-depot.md) and [Hamburg](../entities/hamburg-depot.md), on Tuesdays and Fridays [src: docs/carriers.md].
- **Van Dijk** — dry goods, running daily; the documents do not name which lanes it serves *(unverified)* [src: docs/carriers.md].
- **Baltic Reefer** — chilled goods from [Hamburg](../entities/hamburg-depot.md) to [Gdansk](../entities/gdansk-depot.md) on Mondays; no return day is stated *(unverified)* [src: docs/carriers.md].

## Chilled flow

Because chilled goods move only on Tuesday, Friday and Monday sailings, a chilled consignment can sit outside a cold room over the weekend gap between Friday and Monday, which is what the [cold-chain alarm](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm-response.md) and the 24-hour [returns](../procedures/returns-handling.md) window bear on. The documents do not state transit times, so the size of that gap is not established *(unverified)*.
