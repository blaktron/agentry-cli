# Depot network overview

The network runs four depots: Rotterdam and Hamburg handle chilled goods in cold rooms, Antwerp is dry-goods only with no cold room, and Gdansk is a 2025 addition that also handles dry goods only [src: docs/depots.md].

## Opening times and cold storage

| Depot | Opens | Closes | Cold room |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Rotterdam depot](rotterdam-depot.md) | 06:00 | 22:00 | 40 pallets, 2 to 8 °C |
| [Hamburg depot](hamburg-depot.md) | 05:30 | — | 25 pallets |
| [Antwerp depot](antwerp-depot.md) | 07:00 | — | none |
| [Gdansk depot](gdansk-depot.md) | — | — | dry goods only |

Closing time is given only for Rotterdam (22:00); the other depots' closing hours are not stated in the documents *(unverified)* [src: docs/depots.md].
