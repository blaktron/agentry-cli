# Rotterdam depot

The Rotterdam depot opens at 06:00 and closes at 22:00, and its cold room holds 40 pallets held between 2 and 8 °C [src: docs/depots.md]. It is one of the two chilled-goods depots in the network, alongside [Hamburg](hamburg-depot.md), and is the origin or destination of the [Nordfreight](carrier-network.md) chilled lane.

## Cold room and alarm handling

When the cold room passes 8 °C for ten minutes, the shift lead moves the pallets to a backup cold room on site and calls facilities on extension 4410 [src: docs/alarms.md]. Every alarm is logged in the incident book within the hour [src: docs/alarms.md]. The existence of a backup room alongside the 40-pallet main room is stated only in the alarm procedure, so the backup room's capacity is not known *(unverified)*.

## Staffing

Rotterdam runs three shifts, each with a shift lead and six pickers; new pickers shadow an experienced picker for two weeks [src: docs/staff.md]. See [staffing and shifts](../topics/staffing-and-shifts.md) and the [cold-chain alarm response](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm-response.md).
