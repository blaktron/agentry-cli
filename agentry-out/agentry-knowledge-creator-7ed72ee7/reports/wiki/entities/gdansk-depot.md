# Gdansk depot

The Gdansk depot opened in 2025, making it the newest of the four depots, and handles dry goods only [src: docs/depots.md]. Opening hours and any cold storage are not stated *(unverified)*.

## Lane served

[Baltic Reefer](../topics/carrier-network.md) carries chilled goods from [Hamburg](hamburg-depot.md) to Gdansk on Mondays [src: docs/carriers.md]. The documents name no carrier running dry goods into or out of Gdansk, and no return path for chilled goods arriving there, so how chilled stock is dealt with after arrival is unsettled *(unverified)*.
