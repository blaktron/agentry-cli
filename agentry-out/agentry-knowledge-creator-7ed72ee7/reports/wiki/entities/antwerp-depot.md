# Antwerp depot

The Antwerp depot opens at 07:00, the latest opening in the network, and has no cold room, so it takes no chilled stock [src: docs/depots.md]. Its closing time is not given in the documents *(unverified)*.

## Staffing

Antwerp runs two shifts, fewer than the three at [Rotterdam](rotterdam-depot.md) [src: docs/staff.md]. Because there is no cold room, the [cold-chain alarm](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm-response.md) does not apply at Antwerp; chilled returns could not be held there anyway, and returns are inspected at the depot that shipped them [src: docs/returns.md]. See [returns handling](../procedures/returns-handling.md).
