# Hamburg depot

The Hamburg depot opens at 05:30, the earliest opening in the network, and its cold room holds 25 pallets [src: docs/depots.md]. It handles chilled goods and sits between the [Rotterdam](rotterdam-depot.md) lane and the Baltic lane run by [Baltic Reefer](../topics/carrier-network.md) to [Gdansk](gdansk-depot.md). Closing hours are not given *(unverified)*.

## Night shift and alarm handling

Hamburg's night shift has a night manager instead of a shift lead [src: docs/staff.md]. Unlike Rotterdam's timed escalation, the Hamburg cold-room alarm pages the night manager directly, and the alarm is logged in the incident book within the hour [src: docs/alarms.md]. See [cold-chain alarm response](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm-response.md).

## Fire drill finding

Hamburg was the depot where the last quarterly fire drill found a blocked exit, since cleared [src: docs/safety.md]. Quarterly drills run at every depot; see [depot safety rules](../procedures/depot-safety-rules.md).
