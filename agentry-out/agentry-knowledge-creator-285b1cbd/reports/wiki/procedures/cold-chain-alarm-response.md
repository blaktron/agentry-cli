# Cold-chain alarm response

The cold-chain alarm procedure covers a cold room drifting above its chilled range. It is documented for [Rotterdam depot](../entities/rotterdam-depot.md) and, differently, for [Hamburg depot](../entities/hamburg-depot.md). [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]

## Trigger and actions at Rotterdam

- **Trigger:** the Rotterdam cold room passes 8 °C for ten minutes. [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]
- **Relocation:** the shift lead moves the pallets to the backup room. [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]
- **Escalation:** the shift lead calls facilities on extension 4410. [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]

The 8 °C trigger sits at the top of the room's stated 2 to 8 °C holding range, and the room's capacity is 40 pallets. [src: doc:docs/depots.md] The capacity of the backup room is not documented. [src: doc:docs/alarms.md] *(unverified)*

## Actions at Hamburg

At Hamburg the alarm pages the night manager rather than a shift lead, consistent with the night-manager night shift at that site. The documents do not give a Hamburg threshold or a physical response step. See [shifts and staffing](../topics/shifts-and-staffing.md). [src: doc:docs/alarms.md; doc:docs/staff.md] *(unverified)*

## Logging

Every alarm, at any depot, is logged in the incident book within the hour. [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]

## Not covered by the sources

The documents describe no cold room at Antwerp or Gdansk, and so no alarm path for those sites. [src: doc:docs/depots.md] *(unverified)*
