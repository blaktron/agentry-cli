# Rotterdam depot

Rotterdam is one of the network's chilled-goods depots, open from 06:00 to 22:00. Its cold room holds 40 pallets at 2 to 8 °C, the largest cold storage capacity of the depots described. [src: doc:docs/depots.md]

## Cold storage and alarms

The cold room is the site of the network's documented cold-chain alarm rule: if the room passes 8 °C for ten minutes, the shift lead moves the pallets to a backup room and calls facilities on extension 4410, with the alarm logged in the incident book within the hour. See [cold-chain alarm response](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm-response.md). [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]

The existence of a backup room at Rotterdam is stated in the alarm procedure; the backup room's capacity is not given. [src: doc:docs/alarms.md] *(unverified)*

## Staffing

Rotterdam runs three shifts, each led by a shift lead — the role that owns the cold-room response. See [shifts and staffing](../topics/shifts-and-staffing.md). [src: doc:docs/staff.md]

## Connections

Rotterdam is one end of Nordfreight's chilled lane to [Hamburg depot](hamburg-depot.md); Van Dijk carries dry goods daily to the depot. Each carrier presents a temperature log at hand-over. See [carriers and lanes](../topics/carriers-and-lanes.md). [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]

Returns are inspected at the depot that shipped them, so Rotterdam handles its own outbound returns. See [returns handling](../topics/returns-handling.md). [src: doc:docs/returns.md]
