# Hamburg depot

Hamburg opens at 05:30, the earliest opening time of the depots in the network, and has a cold room holding 25 pallets. Its cold-room capacity is smaller than Rotterdam's 40 pallets. [src: doc:docs/depots.md]

## Cold-chain alarms

At Hamburg a cold-room alarm pages the night manager rather than a shift lead, which matches the site's night-manager staffing model. Every alarm is logged in the incident book within the hour. See [cold-chain alarm response](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm-response.md). [src: doc:docs/alarms.md; doc:docs/staff.md]

The Hamburg alarm rule does not state a temperature threshold or duration, unlike the Rotterdam rule of 8 °C for ten minutes. [src: doc:docs/alarms.md] *(unverified)*

## Staffing

Hamburg's night shift is run by a night manager instead of a shift lead. See [shifts and staffing](../topics/shifts-and-staffing.md). [src: doc:docs/staff.md]

## Safety

Hamburg's most recent documented fire drill found a blocked exit, since cleared. See [depot safety rules](../topics/depot-safety.md). [src: doc:docs/safety.md]

## Connections

Hamburg sits between [Rotterdam depot](rotterdam-depot.md), served by Nordfreight's chilled lane on Tuesdays and Fridays, and [Gdansk depot](gdansk-depot.md), reached by Baltic Reefer's chilled service on Mondays; Van Dijk carries dry goods daily. See [carriers and lanes](../topics/carriers-and-lanes.md). [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]
