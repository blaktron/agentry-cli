# Antwerp depot

Antwerp opens at 07:00, the latest opening time of the depots in the network, and has no cold room. Because of that it cannot hold chilled goods in refrigerated storage. [src: doc:docs/depots.md]

## Staffing

Antwerp runs two shifts. The documents do not name a shift-lead count for the site, though each Rotterdam shift has a shift lead and six pickers. See [shifts and staffing](../topics/shifts-and-staffing.md). [src: doc:docs/staff.md] *(unverified)*

## Returns

Antwerp accepts its own returns for inspection, since returns are always inspected at the depot that shipped them and never at another depot. See [returns handling](../topics/returns-handling.md). [src: doc:docs/returns.md]

With no cold room, the chilled-returns window (arrival within 24 hours of dispatch, otherwise destruction) has no documented on-site refrigerated staging point at Antwerp. [src: doc:docs/depots.md; doc:docs/returns.md] *(unverified)*
