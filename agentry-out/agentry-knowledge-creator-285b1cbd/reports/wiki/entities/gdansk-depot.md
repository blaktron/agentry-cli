# Gdansk depot

Gdansk opened in 2025, the most recent depot to join the network, and handles dry goods only. The documents give no opening hours, cold room or shift pattern for the site. [src: doc:docs/depots.md] *(unverified)*

## Chilled inbound tension

The carrier documents describe Baltic Reefer carrying chilled goods from [Hamburg depot](hamburg-depot.md) to Gdansk on Mondays, while the depot documents say Gdansk handles dry goods only. The two statements are not reconciled in the sources. See [carriers and lanes](../topics/carriers-and-lanes.md). [src: doc:docs/carriers.md; doc:docs/depots.md] *(unverified)*

Every carrier must present a temperature log at hand-over, which applies to Baltic Reefer's Hamburg-to-Gdansk movements. [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]
