# Cold-chain alarm procedure

A cold-chain alarm is raised when a depot cold room leaves its chilled band. The response differs by depot. [src: docs/alarms.md]

## Rotterdam

When the [Rotterdam depot](../entities/rotterdam-depot.md) cold room passes 8 °C for ten minutes, the shift lead moves the pallets to the backup room and calls facilities on extension 4410. [src: docs/alarms.md]

## Hamburg

At the [Hamburg depot](../entities/hamburg-depot.md) the alarm pages the night manager. The documents give no Hamburg temperature threshold or dwell time (unverified). [src: docs/alarms.md]

## Logging

Every alarm is logged in the incident book within the hour, at any depot. [src: docs/alarms.md]
