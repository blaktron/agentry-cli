# Hamburg depot

The Hamburg depot is the earliest-opening depot in the network, at 05:30. [src: docs/depots.md]

It has a cold room holding 25 pallets, smaller than the [Rotterdam depot](rotterdam-depot.md) cold room. [src: docs/depots.md]

A cold-chain alarm at Hamburg pages the night manager rather than a shift lead, as described in the [cold-chain alarm procedure](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm.md). [src: docs/alarms.md]
