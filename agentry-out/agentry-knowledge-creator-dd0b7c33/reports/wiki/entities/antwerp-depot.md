# Antwerp depot

The Antwerp depot opens at 07:00, the latest opening of the depots listed. [src: docs/depots.md]

It has no cold room, unlike the [Rotterdam](rotterdam-depot.md) and [Hamburg](hamburg-depot.md) depots, which hold 40 and 25 pallets of chilled stock respectively. Closing time for Antwerp is not given in the documents (unverified). [src: docs/depots.md]
