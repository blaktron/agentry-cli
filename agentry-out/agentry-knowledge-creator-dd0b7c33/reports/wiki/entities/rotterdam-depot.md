# Rotterdam depot

The Rotterdam depot is one of the four depots in the network and the only one with a backup cold room for alarm situations. It opens at 06:00 and closes at 22:00. [src: docs/depots.md]

Its cold room holds 40 pallets and is kept between 2 and 8 °C. [src: docs/depots.md]

When the cold room goes above 8 °C, the [cold-chain alarm procedure](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm.md) applies: the shift lead moves pallets to the backup room and calls facilities. [src: docs/alarms.md]
