# Depot network

Four depots make up the network, each with its own opening time, cold capacity and shift pattern [src: doc:docs/depots.md; doc:docs/staff.md]. Only Rotterdam and Hamburg have cold rooms, so chilled goods move through those two [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

| Depot | Opens | Cold room | Shifts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Rotterdam](rotterdam-depot.md) | 06:00, closes 22:00 | 40 pallets at 2–8 °C | 3 |
| [Antwerp](antwerp-depot.md) | 07:00 | none | 2 |
| [Hamburg](hamburg-depot.md) | 05:30 | 25 pallets | night manager on nights |
| [Gdansk](gdansk-depot.md) | not documented | dry goods only | not documented |

- Rotterdam is the only depot whose closing time is documented (22:00); Antwerp, Hamburg and Gdansk closing times are not stated (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md].
- Gdansk is the newest, opened in 2025 [src: doc:docs/depots.md].
- Chilled lanes connect Rotterdam and Hamburg, and Hamburg and Gdansk; Van Dijk carries dry goods daily across the network [src: doc:docs/carriers.md].
- Fire drills run each quarter at every depot [src: doc:docs/safety.md].

See the quick reference [depot hours and capacity](../references/depot-hours-and-capacity.md), and [staffing and shifts](../topics/staffing-and-shifts.md) for how depots are crewed.
