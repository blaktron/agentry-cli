# Rotterdam depot

The Rotterdam depot is the network's chilled-goods hub, open from 06:00 to 22:00 [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. It runs three shifts, each with a shift lead and six pickers [src: doc:docs/staff.md].

## Cold room

- Holds 40 pallets at 2 to 8 °C [src: doc:docs/depots.md].
- If the room passes 8 °C for ten minutes, the shift lead moves the pallets to a backup room and calls facilities on extension 4410 [src: doc:docs/alarms.md].
- The backup room exists for that overflow, and cold-room work over 10 minutes needs insulated gloves [src: doc:docs/alarms.md; doc:docs/safety.md].

## Operations

Rotterdam is the origin or destination of the chilled Nordfreight lane to Hamburg, on Tuesdays and Fridays [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]. Returns are inspected at the depot that shipped them, so Rotterdam-shipped returns come back to Rotterdam [src: doc:docs/returns.md]. See [depot network](depot-network.md), [cold-chain alarm handling](../topics/cold-chain-alarms.md) and [staffing and shifts](../topics/staffing-and-shifts.md).
