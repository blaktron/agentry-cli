# Hamburg depot

The Hamburg depot opens at 05:30, the earliest opening in the network; its documented closing time is not stated (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. It has a cold room holding 25 pallets — fewer than Rotterdam's 40 — and its refrigerated temperature range is not stated (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

## Staffing and alarms

- The night shift has a night manager instead of a shift lead [src: doc:docs/staff.md].
- A cold-chain alarm at Hamburg pages the night manager rather than calling a shift lead [src: doc:docs/alarms.md].
- Alarms are logged in the incident book within the hour [src: doc:docs/alarms.md].

## Lanes

Baltic Reefer carries chilled goods from Hamburg to Gdansk on Mondays, and Nordfreight runs chilled between Rotterdam and Hamburg on Tuesdays and Fridays [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]. See [depot network](depot-network.md) and [cold-chain alarm handling](../topics/cold-chain-alarms.md).
