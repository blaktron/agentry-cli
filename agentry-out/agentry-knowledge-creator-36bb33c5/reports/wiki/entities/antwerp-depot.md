# Antwerp depot

The Antwerp depot opens at 07:00 — the latest opening documented in the network — and has no cold room [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. Its closing time is not stated in the documents (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

With no cold room, Antwerp has no cold-chain alarm of its own and is not named on any chilled carrier lane (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md; doc:docs/alarms.md; doc:docs/carriers.md]. It runs two shifts, against Rotterdam's three [src: doc:docs/staff.md]. Its documented closing time, goods profile and shift lead arrangements are not stated (unverified) [src: doc:docs/staff.md].

See [depot network](depot-network.md) and [staffing and shifts](../topics/staffing-and-shifts.md).
