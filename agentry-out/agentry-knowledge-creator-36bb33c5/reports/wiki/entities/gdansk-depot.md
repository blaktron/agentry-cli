# Gdansk depot

The Gdansk depot opened in 2025 and handles dry goods only [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. It is the newest depot in the network, and no opening hours, cold room or shift pattern is documented for it (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

Its one documented inbound lane is Baltic Reefer, which carries chilled goods from Hamburg to Gdansk on Mondays [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]. Since the depot handles dry goods only, the chilled Hamburg lane and the dry-goods profile sit in tension — whether chilled goods are accepted at Gdansk is unresolved in the documents (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md; doc:docs/carriers.md].

See [depot network](depot-network.md).
