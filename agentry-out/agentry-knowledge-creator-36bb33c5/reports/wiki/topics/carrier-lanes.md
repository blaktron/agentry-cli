# Carrier lanes

Three carriers serve the depots, split between chilled and dry freight, and each must present a temperature log at hand-over [src: doc:docs/carriers.md].

- **Nordfreight** — chilled goods between [Rotterdam](../entities/rotterdam-depot.md) and [Hamburg](../entities/hamburg-depot.md), on Tuesdays and Fridays [src: doc:docs/carriers.md].
- **Van Dijk** — dry goods daily; the only carrier documented as running every day [src: doc:docs/carriers.md].
- **Baltic Reefer** — chilled goods from [Hamburg](../entities/hamburg-depot.md) to [Gdansk](../entities/gdansk-depot.md) on Mondays [src: doc:docs/carriers.md].

The chilled lanes only touch depots with cold rooms, Rotterdam and Hamburg, though Baltic Reefer delivers chilled goods to Gdansk, a dry-goods-only depot — whether Gdansk accepts chilled deliveries is unresolved in the documents (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md; doc:docs/carriers.md]. Temperature logs at hand-over tie carrier paperwork to the cold-room limits described in [cold-chain alarm handling](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm-handling.md) [src: doc:docs/carriers.md; doc:docs/depots.md].
