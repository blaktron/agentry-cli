# Rotterdam depot

Rotterdam is a chilled-goods depot open 06:00 to 22:00, and the site with the network's largest cold room. It runs three shifts, each with a shift lead and six pickers [src: doc:docs/depots.md; doc:docs/staff.md].

## Cold room

The cold room holds 40 pallets at 2 to 8 °C [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. If it passes 8 °C for ten minutes, the shift lead moves the pallets to the backup room and calls facilities on extension 4410, and the alarm is logged in the incident book within the hour [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]. Insulated gloves are required for cold-room work lasting more than ten minutes [src: doc:docs/safety.md]. See [cold-chain alarm response](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm.md).

## Freight and returns

Nordfreight carries chilled goods between Rotterdam and the [Hamburg depot](hamburg-depot.md) on Tuesdays and Fridays, presenting a temperature log at hand-over [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]. Returns are inspected at the depot that shipped them, so chilled goods dispatched from Rotterdam come back to Rotterdam; chilled returns must arrive within 24 hours of dispatch or are destroyed [src: doc:docs/returns.md]. See [returns handling](../procedures/returns-handling.md).
