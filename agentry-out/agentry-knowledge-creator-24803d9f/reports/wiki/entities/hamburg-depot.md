# Hamburg depot

Hamburg opens at 05:30, the earliest of the depots, and runs a cold room of 25 pallets [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. Its night shift is staffed differently from the other sites: a night manager rather than a shift lead [src: doc:docs/staff.md].

## Cold chain

A cold-room alarm at Hamburg pages the night manager, instead of the phone escalation used at [Rotterdam depot](rotterdam-depot.md), and the alarm is entered in the incident book within the hour [src: doc:docs/alarms.md]. See [cold-chain alarm response](../procedures/cold-chain-alarm.md). Cold-room work beyond ten minutes requires insulated gloves [src: doc:docs/safety.md].

## Lanes and drills

Baltic Reefer carries chilled goods from Hamburg to the [Gdansk depot](gdansk-depot.md) on Mondays, and Nordfreight runs chilled goods between Hamburg and Rotterdam on Tuesdays and Fridays; each carrier presents a temperature log at hand-over [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]. The last quarterly fire drill at Hamburg found a blocked exit, since cleared [src: doc:docs/safety.md].
