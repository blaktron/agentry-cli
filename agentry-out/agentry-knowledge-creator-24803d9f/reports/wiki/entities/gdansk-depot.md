# Gdansk depot

Gdansk is the newest depot, opened in 2025, and handles dry goods only [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. It has no recorded cold room, consistent with its dry-goods scope [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

## Chilled arrivals

Despite that scope, Baltic Reefer carries chilled goods from the [Hamburg depot](hamburg-depot.md) to Gdansk on Mondays, presenting a temperature log at hand-over [src: doc:docs/carriers.md]. The documents do not say where those chilled goods are stored once in Gdansk, nor Gdansk's opening hours or shift pattern (unverified) [src: doc:docs/depots.md; doc:docs/staff.md]. Dry goods are accepted as returns for 30 days at the depot that shipped them [src: doc:docs/returns.md].
