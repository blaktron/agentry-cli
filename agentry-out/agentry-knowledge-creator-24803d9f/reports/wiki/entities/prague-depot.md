# Prague depot

Prague's cold room holds 30 pallets at 2 to 8 °C, between Rotterdam's 40 and Hamburg's 25 [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. The depot closes at 21:00, earlier than the 22:00 closing recorded for the rest of the network [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

## What is not recorded

The documents give no opening time, shift pattern or carrier lane for Prague, and no cold-chain alarm rule for it, although refrigerated storage in the 2 to 8 °C band is what the alarm procedure protects elsewhere [src: doc:docs/depots.md; doc:docs/staff.md; doc:docs/alarms.md; doc:docs/carriers.md]. Chilled goods returning to Prague must arrive within 24 hours of dispatch or are destroyed, since returns are inspected at the depot that shipped them [src: doc:docs/returns.md]. See [returns handling](../procedures/returns-handling.md).
