# Antwerp depot

Antwerp opens at 07:00, the latest opening of the depots, and has no cold room at all [src: doc:docs/depots.md]. Chilled goods therefore depend on the refrigerated rooms at [Rotterdam depot](rotterdam-depot.md) and [Hamburg depot](hamburg-depot.md) rather than Antwerp [src: doc:docs/depots.md].

## Shifts

The site runs two shifts, fewer than Rotterdam's three [src: doc:docs/staff.md]. The documents do not state Antwerp's closing time or its pallet capacity (unverified). No cold-chain alarm rule is recorded for Antwerp, which is consistent with its lack of a cold room [src: doc:docs/alarms.md].
