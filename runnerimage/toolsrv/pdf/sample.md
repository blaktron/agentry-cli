# The harbour works: a review of the first year

*What was built, what it cost and what comes next*

## Summary

The first year of the harbour works finished on time and within its budget. The new quay wall is complete along its full length, the dredging reached the planned depth in all three basins, and the two cranes ordered for the container berth arrived in the spring. This review sets out what was built, what it cost, and the decisions the board will take before the second year begins.

## What was built

| Work | Planned | Done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quay wall | 420 m | 420 m |
| Dredging | 3 basins | 3 basins |
| Cranes | 2 | 2 |
| Rail spur | 1.2 km | 0.8 km |

The rail spur is the one piece behind its plan: the land along its last stretch was bought in the autumn, three months later than planned.

> The quay wall is the work the rest depends on, and it is done.

## What it cost

The works cost 41.2 million in the year against a budget of 43.0 million. The saving came from the dredging, where the ground was softer than the survey assumed, and it will meet the rail spur's extra cost in the second year.

## What comes next

The second year finishes the rail spur, paves the container yard and opens the berth to its first ships in the autumn. The board will decide in the first quarter whether to start the second quay wall a year early.
